# Tokens are a resource, like money and time

*Pranav Garg, cofounder of Kolo. Posted on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pranav6082/) on 30 October 2026.*

AI didn't change the fundamental rule in business. Resources - money, time and now tokens - each one has to move the work, then the product, then the company.

Engineering bandwidth, for example, was always tightly managed.

A strong engineer/PM/designer would ask why they or the team should spend time on whatever was asked, and push back.

Today, AI obviously doesn't push back, so the onus is on whoever is spending tokens.

I got this wrong too.

In April, I was working on a lead acquisition attribution flow. It burned through about ₹68k worth of tokens. It did not move anything meaningful.

The AI factory I built did move the work. But it had a token efficiency issue. It burned through $2k of tokens in a day.

Token spend moving the outcome in proportion is the core, and that's the hard part.

The fundamental rule of what to work on, and how much to spend for the outcome, plays out the same way here.
